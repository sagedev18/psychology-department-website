                                        <span class="course-sequence">9</span>
                                        <span class="course-code">AIPSY 302</span>
                                        <span class="course-title">Generative AI and Agentic Systems for Psychological Practice</span>
                                        <span class="course-meta">Level 300 · Second Semester · 2 Units · Compulsory · Lecture 30 h / Practical 45 h</span>
                                    </div>
                                    <div class="description-body">
                                        <h4>Technology focus</h4>
                                        <p>This course brings the generative-AI and AI-agent frontier into psychology: how large language and multimodal models work, reliably prompting, grounding models in trusted sources through retrieval, and building constrained, tool-using agents always under human authority and verification.</p>
                                        <h4>Toolbox</h4>
                                        <table>
                                            <tr><th>Tool / technology</th><th>What the student uses it for</th></tr>
                                            <tr><td>LLM APIs & assistants (Claude, GPT, Gemini)</td><td>Generation, reasoning and drafting</td></tr>
                                            <tr><td>Prompt-engineering patterns</td><td>Role, context, examples and constraints</td></tr>
                                            <tr><td>Retrieval-augmented generation (vector stores)</td><td>Ground answers in trusted documents and data</td></tr>
                                            <tr><td>LangGraph / LangChain</td><td>Compose tool-using, multi-step agents</td></tr>
                                            <tr><td>Model Context Protocol (MCP)</td><td>Connect agents safely to tools and data</td></tr>
                                            <tr><td>Streamlit</td><td>Wrap an assistant or agent in a usable app</td></tr>
                                        </table>
                                        <h4>Practical lab drills</h4>
                                        <ul>
                                            <li>Engineer a prompt that drafts a psychology work product, then verify every claim.</li>
                                            <li>Build a retrieval assistant grounded in psychological literature, clinical guidelines and ethical codes.</li>
                                            <li>Build a constrained agent that uses a tool to complete a multi-step task.</li>
                                            <li>Add a human-in-the-loop checkpoint and test it.</li>
                                            <li>Red team your assistant: induce a hallucination and add a guardrail.</li>
                                        </ul>
                                        <h4>Identification skills</h4>
                                        <ul>
                                            <li>recognize hallucination, fabricated citations and unsafe agent actions.</li>
                                            <li>Identify where retrieval grounding is required and generation alone is unsafe.</li>
                                            <li>Judge which tasks may be delegated to an agent, and which must not.</li>
                                        </ul>
                                        <h4>Local application and startup lens</h4>
                                        <p>Students apply generative and agentic AI to real psychology tasks drafting reports and psychoeducational materials, supporting (not replacing) practitioners, and prototyping wellbeing copilots with safeguards including English- and Igbo-language outputs. Startup lens: vertical AI copilots for Nigerian psychology workflows are a fast-emerging venture class.</p>
                                        <h4>Ethics and professional responsibility</h4>
                                        <p>The course confronts hallucination, automation bias, intellectual property and licensing under the Copyright Act 2022, and confidentiality under the NDPA 2023. The governing principle is human authority: the practitioner remains accountable for any output or action.</p>
                                        <h4>Learning outcomes</h4>
                                        <p>On completion, the student should be able to:</p>
                                        <ul>
                                            <li>Explain how generative and agentic AI work and where they fail.</li>
                                            <li>Engineer reliable prompts and retrieval-grounded assistants.</li>
                                            <li>Build a constrained, tool-using agent with human-in-the-loop safeguards.</li>
                                            <li>Apply intellectual-property, confidentiality and accountability principles.</li>
                                        </ul>
                                        <h4>Course contents</h4>
                                        <p>Foundations of generative AI: large language and multimodal models, tokens, context windows and limits. Prompt engineering and hallucination mitigation. Retrieval-augmented generation: embeddings, vector stores and grounding in trusted sources. AI agents: tool use, planning, the Model Context Protocol and human-in-the-loop control. Building and securing agents and simple application interfaces. Intellectual property under the Copyright Act 2022 and confidentiality under the NDPA 2023. Laboratory and project work throughout.</p>
