Overview

Enugu State where Enugu State University of Science and Technology is located could be described as a melting pot of the Igbo race because of its historic status as the capital of South-eastern States during the colonial and post-colonial periods. This historic status has led to ceaseless influx of people into Enugu State. This amalgamation of people from other States despite its benefits contributes to the current wave of suspicion and social upheavals due to intra and interpersonal conflict, communal clashes, perceptions of marginalization and the trending agitations of the Indigenous People of Biafra (IPOB). To this end, these consequences have affected the harmony and inter-communal relations among Enugu State (Coal City) residents resulting in poor socio-economic development and psychological well-being.

Psychology of Harmony and Inter-communal Relations will equip Psychology graduates with the necessary skills to become advocates of peace that will play a significant role in assisting individuals, communities and the entire society to thrive since peace is sine qua non to development. Psychology of harmony and inter-communal relations is the study of mental processes and behavior that promote agreement of ideas, feelings and actions which enhance fairness, respect, dignity for the purpose of making conflict a less likely occurrence and helping to heal its psychological effects. This course also seeks to develop theory and practices that prevent conflict and violence, and mitigate the effects they have on the society. The course contents, objectives and learning outcomes will equip students of psychology with the relevant psychological skills to build harmonious and healthy inter-communal relations among residents of Enugu State. Thus, the course will create conducive environment for growth and development.

Objectives

The objectives of the course are to:

  • compare the various theories of peace and conflict management;
  • identify the roles of principles of psychology in peace and harmony;
  • explain the immediate and remote causes of inter-communal conflicts in Nigeria especially Enugu State;
  • describe the psychological factors enhancing harmony and inter-communal relations;
  • explain the roles of urbanization and attitude change in building a peaceful and harmonious society;
  • describe the psychological factors which enhance unity in diversity; and
  • identify the intervention approaches to promote inter-communal harmony and cohesion in Nigeria.

Learning Outcomes

On the completion of the course, the student should be able to:

  • state at least three theories of peace and conflict management;
  • identify at least three roles of principles of psychology in building peace and harmony;
  • explain at least five immediate and remote causes of inter-communal conflicts in Nigeria especially Enugu State;
  • describe at least five psychological factors enhancing harmony and inter-communal relations;
  • explain at least two roles of attitude change in building peaceful society;
  • describe at least five psychological factors which enhance unity in diversity; and
  • identify at least three intervention approaches to promote inter-communal harmony and cohesion in Nigeria.
