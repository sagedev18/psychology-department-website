About Us

  • Home
  • About Us

Motto

Technology for service.

Mission Statement of the University

To produce graduates that can favorably compete with their counterparts all over the world and to promote scholarship, especially in the areas of Science, Management and Technology, thereby ensuring the development of quality manpower that will utilize technology for the service of the society.

Vision Statement of the University

To be an outstanding internationally respected University in Africa with strong commitment to rigorous scholarship that promotes service to humanity through quality teaching, research and community social responsibility.

History

The Department of Psychology at ESUT was established to train competent and socially responsible psychologists who can understand human behaviour, promote mental wellbeing, and contribute meaningfully to the development of society. Over time, the department has developed a strong academic identity built on research, teaching excellence, and community engagement.

Objectives

  • Instil a sense of enthusiasm towards and identity with psychology as a profession.
  • Appreciate its application in different contexts and environments, and involve students in an intellectually stimulating and satisfying experience of learning and studying.
  • Provide a broad and balanced foundation of psychological knowledge and practical skills through training in psychological methods of investigation such as observation, experiment, case study, computer application and many others.
  • Develop in students the ability to apply their psychological knowledge and skills in proffering solutions to theoretical and practical problems of society.
  • Develop a range of transferable skills of value in physical, social, organizational, industrial, educational, health and cultural environments using generative and creative methods of social engineering.
  • Provide knowledge and skills with which students can proceed to further studies and careers in specialized areas of psychology or multi-disciplinary areas involving psychology.
  • Inculcate in the students the principles and practices that enhance psychological wellbeing.
  • Equip students with the culture of innovation and critical thinking for entrepreneurship.

Unique Features

  • Development of pragmatic skills for effective application of psychological principles for problem-solving in diverse areas of human endeavour.
  • Inculcating ethical behaviours in research and psychological intervention.
  • Developing positive attitude to life for psychosocial well-being.
  • Developing crisis intervention and terror management skills.
  • De-emphasising the teaching of already established scientific/empirical findings, thereby encouraging rational thinking, self-motivated exploration, and learning.
