4-Year Programme (Bachelor of Science in Psychology)

A four-year degree programme for candidates admitted through UTME with the requisite O’Level credits.

Duration: 4 Years

3-Year Programme (Direct Entry)

A three-year degree programme for candidates with appropriate A’Level qualifications or equivalent.

Duration: 3 Years

Postgraduate Programs

Postgraduate Diploma (PGD) in Psychology

Duration: Minimum of 2 Semesters

Master of Science (MSc) in Psychology

Duration: Full-time/Part-time as specified by regulations

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Psychology

Duration: Full-time/Part-time as specified by regulations

Admission Requirements

For a four-year course: In addition to UTME score, the candidate should possess a Senior School Certificate with at least five credit passes, including Mathematics, English and Biology at not more than two sittings.

For the three-year programme: Candidates shall possess five SSC credit passes, two of which must be at Advanced Level. For emphasis, English, Mathematics, and any of the following: Biology, Health Science, Agricultural Science, Animal Husbandry and Economics are required at credit passes. Candidates with ND, NCE, Nursing and Midwifery qualifications are also eligible.

Graduation Requirements

  • Completed and passed all the courses registered for, including all compulsory and required courses as prescribed by the University/Faculty.
  • Obtained a minimum CGPA specified by the University but not less than 1.0.
  • Earned the minimum credit units of not less than 120 for a four-year programme and 90 for a three-year programme.
