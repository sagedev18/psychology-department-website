5 ESUT-PSY 106 Psychology of African Belief System (2 Units; Core; LH= 30)

Senate-approved relevance

To produce psychology graduates who are highly knowledgeable in the Psychology of African Belief systems in accordance with the vision and mission of the Enugu State University of Science and Technology to utilize technology and learning outcomes for service to the society. Thus, Psychology of African Belief System will provide graduates of psychology with skills for the purpose of utilizing such in religious, social, educational, industrial and organizational, family, community and other related institutions for proper human relations, understanding, adjustment and harmonious coexistence in Enugu State and beyond.

Overview

Psychology of African Belief system is a relevant course that all the students of psychology should take part in. This course will equip the students with the knowledge of the psychological principles such as psychological theories, personality, perception, attitude, value, need, emotion, motivation, attribution, locus of control underlying African belief system. The course will also consider the roles of socio-economic factors such as culture, social class, educational qualification, income in African belief system. The course will also acquit the students with application of research methods in psychology in order to enhance their data gathering, analysis and interpretation skills for proper training in this area of psychology.

This course is apt on the heels of the current paradigm shift from Western religion adherence to traditional religion. This trend has caught not only Enugu State residents but other societies as well. The course objectives, learning outcomes and contents to an extent will equip graduates of psychology with basic principles of psychology especially paradigms and research methods in psychology necessary in addressing the various inter-group relations issues such as inter-religious conflicts, hence engendering harmonious coexistence in Enugu State.

Objectives

The objectives of this course are to:

  • explain the concept of African belief system;
  • describe the roles of psychological factors in African belief system;
  • identify the socio-economic factors influencing African belief system;
  • state the relevant theories in understanding African belief system;
  • state the research methods in the study of African belief system; and
  • describe the applications of the outcomes of the course to engender harmonious coexistence in Enugu State.

Learning Outcomes

On completion of the course, the students should be able to:

  • describe at least five roles of psychological factors in African belief system;
  • identify at least three socio-economic factors influencing African belief system;
  • state at least three relevant theories in understanding African belief system;
  • state at least three research methods in the study of African belief system; and
  • describe at least three applications of the outcomes of the course to engender harmonious coexistence in Enugu State.

Course Contents

Overview of African belief systems. African religions and spirituality. Psychology of deity. Psychological theories and African belief system. Personality and African belief system. Theories of personality. Perception and African belief system. Theories of perception. Emotion and African belief system. Value and African belief system. Needs and African belief system. Need theories. Attitude and African belief system. Theories of attitude. Motivation and African belief system. Attribution and locus of control in African belief system. Culture and African belief system. Socio-economic factors and African belief system. Research methods in psychology.
