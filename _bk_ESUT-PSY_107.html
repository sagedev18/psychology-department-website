Overview

Communication plays a vital role in human life. It helps facilitate the exchange of information and knowledge, and develop relationships with others. Nowadays, educational e-content can be found anywhere, anytime and to anyone who can connect to the internet.

In view of this, information and communication psychology uses the internet in order to create programmes for psychological intervention, assessment, orientation, and specialized counseling, as means of prevention. The internet not only constitutes a new communicative medium between client and therapist but is also the future of psychology in the technology world like ours. This new virtual environment uses such tools as e-mail, chat rooms, discussion forums and audio and video conference for the communication and interaction of the therapist with the client, tools which are also used for educational purposes in e-learning environments.

Objectives

The objectives of the course are to:

  • describe processes of online identity and relationships leading to good human relations;
  • state functions of communication;
  • identify the psychological characteristics necessary for human relations;
  • identify important skills such as listening to the other party with credibility and impartiality;
  • explain psychological factors in communication;
  • identify the detrimental effects of social media on behaviour; and
  • describe ethical issues in the use of information and communication technology.

Learning Outcomes

On completion of the course the students should be able to:

  • describe at least three processes of online identity and relationships leading to good human relations;
  • state at least five functions of communication;
  • identify at least five psychological characteristics necessary for human relations;
  • identify at least three important skills in communication;
  • explain five psychological factors in communication;
  • identify at least five detrimental effects of social media on behavior; and
  • describe at least five ethical issues in the use of information and communication technology.
