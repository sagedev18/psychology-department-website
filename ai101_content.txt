                                        <span class="course-code">AIPSY 101</span>
                                        <span class="course-title">Digital Methods and Data Foundations for Psychology</span>
                                        <span class="course-meta">Level 100 · First Semester · 2 Units · Compulsory · Lecture 15 h / Practical 45 h</span>
                                    </div>
                                    <div class="description-body">
                                        <h4>Technology focus</h4>
                                        <p>This course installs the modern data toolchain and reframes the psychologist as a quantitative, reproducible analyst of behavioral data.</p>
                                        <h4>Toolbox</h4>
                                        <table>
                                            <tr><th>Tool / technology</th><th>What the student uses it for</th></tr>
                                            <tr><td>R / RStudio (or JASP/jamovi)</td><td>Reproducible behavioral-data analysis</td></tr>
                                            <tr><td>Python, Jupyter / Colab</td><td>Data analysis and ML readiness</td></tr>
                                            <tr><td>Pandas / tidyverse</td><td>Clean and structure psychological data</td></tr>
                                            <tr><td>Git and GitHub</td><td>Version analyses and materials</td></tr>
                                            <tr><td>OSF & open-data repositories</td><td>Open science and responsible reuse</td></tr>
                                            <tr><td>Survey tools (export to data)</td><td>Structured questionnaire datasets</td></tr>
                                        </table>
                                        <h4>Practical lab drills</h4>
                                        <ul>
                                            <li>Build reproducible R/Python environments and recover them from a clean clone.</li>
                                            <li>Clean a questionnaire dataset and chart responses honestly.</li>
                                            <li>Score a scale and compute reliability correctly.</li>
                                            <li>Version an analysis and pre-register an analytic plan.</li>
                                            <li>Summarize data with appropriate statistics and uncertainty.</li>
                                        </ul>
                                        <h4>Identification skills</h4>
                                        <ul>
                                            <li>Distinguish raw, scored and derived psychological data.</li>
                                            <li>Recognize misleading psychological charts and statistics.</li>
                                            <li>Identify implausible, careless or duplicated responses.</li>
                                        </ul>
                                        <h4>Local application and startup lens</h4>
                                        <p>Skills are anchored to Nigerian psychological realities mental-health, educational and organizational data in Enugu and the South-East.</p>
                                        <h4>Ethics and professional responsibility</h4>
                                        <p>The course introduces analytical honesty, reproducibility and respondent welfare as professional duties under the ethical code of the Nigerian Psychological Association.</p>
                                        <h4>Learning outcomes</h4>
                                        <p>On completion, the student should be able to:</p>
                                        <ul>
                                            <li>Build reproducible, version-controlled analyses of psychological data.</li>
                                            <li>Clean, score and summarize behavioral data honestly.</li>
                                            <li>Compute reliability and report uncertainty.</li>
                                            <li>State the data-protection duties including sensitive data bearing on psychological data.</li>
                                        </ul>
                                        <h4>Course contents</h4>
                                        <p>Data and digital literacy for psychologists: data types, scoring and reproducibility. R/RStudio (or JASP/jamovi), Python and notebooks. Cleaning and structuring questionnaires and behavioral data. Reliability and descriptive statistics. Version control and pre-registration; open science.</p>
                                    </div>
