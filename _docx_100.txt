 1132| Overview
 1133| Suicide is death caused by injuring oneself with the intent to die. It is the act of voluntarily causing one’s own death. Suicide poses major risks to public health worldwide. Human suicide has been reported for more than 3000 years for social knowledge. In the ancient Greece, Egypt, Rome, Africa and even Igbo tribe, suicide was not allowed and regarded as sinful.
